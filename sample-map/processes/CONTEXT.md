# Process Shelf Contract

## Reads

- the selected process card;
- its linked object cards;
- only the subject sources cited by the process.

## Does

Maps one movement proven to run. A process card states Input → Movement → Output with citations.

## Writes

One process card. Do not create a card for aspiration or a narrative tour.

## Human check

Verify every step against source and confirm that `consumes` and `produces` links resolve.
