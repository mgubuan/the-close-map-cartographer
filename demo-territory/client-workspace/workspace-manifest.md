# Cedar Ridge Home Services — July Close Workspace

This privacy-safe territory is a sanitized composite of recurring monthly-close work in bookkeeping firms. `Cedar Ridge Home Services` is fictional; identifiers, dates, and record contents are synthetic. The workflow boundaries, handoffs, and naming collisions reflect real operating patterns.

## Engagement

- Workspace ID: `CRHS-042`
- Service: monthly accrual-basis bookkeeping and management reporting
- Close period: `2026-07`
- Target: reviewer approval by business day 10
- Accounting system: QuickBooks Online
- Preparer role: Senior Bookkeeper
- Reviewer role: Client Accounting Services Manager
- Client contact role: Operations Manager

## Connected surfaces

- Client portal: statements, payroll reports, loan statements, and requested support
- Controlled intake email: vendor documents forwarded by the client
- QuickBooks Online: ledger, reconciliations, and close-period work records
- Payroll provider: payroll register and cash-requirement report
- Merchant processor: settlement report

## Live controls

- `source-document-index.csv` is the evidence identity and receipt log.
- `exception-register.csv` is the live missing-item and clarification queue.
- `review-packet-2026-07.md` is the bounded preparer-to-reviewer handoff.
- `approval-register.csv` is the only approval authority.

The workspace boundary includes only records carrying both `CRHS-042` and `2026-07`. Client values, credentials, tax IDs, full account numbers, and actual documents are deliberately absent.
