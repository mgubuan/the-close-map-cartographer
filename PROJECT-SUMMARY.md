# What This Gives a Bookkeeping Firm

The Close Map turns a monthly-close folder into a source-linked guide a new bookkeeper can use without a long handoff call. The reader starts with one proposed change, opens one relevant control, sees what else is affected and what can stay complete, verifies the answer against source, and stops.

When the firm changes a deadline, review rule, reconciliation frequency, required tie-out, or delivery procedure, the guide shows which related work must also change and which work can remain untouched.

The included July close is a privacy-safe reconstruction of recurring close work directly operated and supervised during more than twenty years of controllership. Its client identity and record contents are synthetic, and it contains no client values. Try the live version at https://intelligencesolved.com/close-map.
